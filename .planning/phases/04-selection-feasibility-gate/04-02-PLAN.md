## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| native selection event → range utility | Platform-supplied episode/revision/offset fields are untrusted before reconstruction. |
| synthetic fixture → physical evidence | Only committed synthetic story content may be copied into evidence. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-02-TM | Tampering | `reconstructEpisodeSelection` | high | mitigate | Validate episode ID, document revision, integer ordering, and bounds before direct slicing; reject every malformed/stale case in unit tests. |
| T-04-02-ID | Information Disclosure | `episodeSelectionFixture.ts` | medium | mitigate | Use authored synthetic content only and prohibit persistence, transport, user identifiers, and production logs. |
| T-04-02-RP | Repudiation | exact-range evidence inputs | low | accept | Deterministic fixture ID/revision and exact unit expectations make local reconstruction reproducible without an external audit system. |
</threat_model>
