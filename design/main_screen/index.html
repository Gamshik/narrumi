Context-English MVP Design

Interactive Series Hub and non-SRS Dictionary mockup

Main Screen Design Notes

Interactive specifications for the new Series-First learning flow and the redesigned Dictionary.

1. METRICS & CARD BACKLOGS REMOVED:
No study queues, review metrics, streak counts, or review debt.
The user's progress is measured purely by reading stories.

2. SERIES DETAILS VIEW (OFFLINE HISTORY):
Clicking any series card displays its description, current memory, and a list of all completed episodes.
Completed episodes can be reopened and read/listened offline in read-only mode.

3. PREPARE EPISODE SCREEN (SWAP/SHUFFLE):
Before generation, the user is presented with suggested words.
- Clicking [✕ Swap] dynamically fades the word out, queries the offline dictionary database, and spring-slides a new word in.
- Clicking [✓ Use] confirms focus target inclusion.
- Option: "Generate Episode" (AI writer/validator pipeline online).

4. DICTIONARY AS PICKER SOURCE:
The dictionary drops SRS categories. All filters are shifted to:
All | Used in series | Translated | Known | Later.
Words carrying these states are queried by the picker to recommend future story words.

AI Series Specifications

Length & Level Constraints:

Episodes stay concise enough for a comfortable learning session, while remaining substantial enough to develop the scene and interaction. Length adapts to the story and CEFR level. Grammar fits strictly within manual CEFR selections [A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 | C1].

Memory & Feedback rules:

Compacted series summaries prevent LLM context bloat. Story corrections are brief and non-punitive to preserve reader immersion.
